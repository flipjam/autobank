# Autobank Mission

**Status:** Owner-approved objective; workspace bootstrap in progress  
**Current revenue stage:** Stage 1 — first verified dollar  
**Current operating phase:** Setup and validation  
**External actions:** Disabled by `CONTROL.json`

## Primary objective

Maximize lawful, truthful, sustainable **net revenue** with Pi performing as much of the work as practical through the approved local inference box and zero-cost routine operation.

The owner remains responsible for identity, legal agreements, financial accounts, taxes, payment movement, explicit approvals, and other actions that cannot or should not be delegated.

## Ordered milestones

1. Earn the first verified **$1**.
2. Reach **$100 cumulative net revenue**.
3. Establish repeatable monthly net revenue.
4. Scale the strongest validated opportunities.

Later milestones must not distract from the current one unless evidence shows that a different near-term action materially improves the probability or speed of achieving the current milestone.

## Current setup mission

Before any revenue campaign begins:

1. Validate the repository operating framework and deterministic checks.
2. Verify Pi can read, update, commit, push, stop, and resume durable state.
3. Verify `pi-web-access` uses an approved non-metered configuration with no silent paid fallback.
4. Verify Agent Browser supports safe read-only public browsing and clean session shutdown.
5. Verify the local inference endpoint is the only routine unattended model backend.
6. Verify owner requests and emergency-stop behavior.
7. Produce a bounded opportunity-research plan for owner activation.

## First-dollar strategy requirements

The initial revenue campaign must:

- Require no autonomous spending.
- Have a credible path to actual payment rather than only traffic or attention.
- Fit Pi's strengths in research, software, documentation, testing, and iterative execution.
- Use only authorized accounts, tools, and platforms.
- Have a bounded effort budget and explicit stop, continue, and pivot criteria.
- Prefer a short feedback loop and external evidence over speculative long-term building.
- Preserve the ability to change lanes when evidence weakens.

## Definitions

**Verified revenue** means real payment credited to an owner-approved account and withdrawable or subject only to ordinary platform payout timing, with a corresponding ledger entry and evidence record.

**Net revenue** means gross revenue minus direct platform fees, processing fees, approved expenses, refunds, and chargebacks. Taxes withheld are tracked separately, along with net cash after withholding.

**Experiment success** is defined by the active experiment before work begins. Activity, code volume, listings, proposals, views, downloads, and expected payments are not revenue and are not automatically success.

## Non-goals during setup

- No external submissions, postings, listings, purchases, account creation, outreach, or financial activity.
- No open-ended product build.
- No use of paid or metered services by the unattended runtime.
- No expansion of Pi's authority.

Any change to this mission's objective, milestone order, or setup boundary requires explicit owner approval and a committed update before Pi acts under it.
